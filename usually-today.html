Present Simple vs Present Continuous

Сначала прочитай карточки. Если в заданиях засомневаешься, возвращайся сюда.

Present Simple

обычно, всегда, привычки, факты, расписание
usuallyalwaysoftenneverevery dayon Mondays
+I / you / we / they work
he / she / it works
−I don’t work
she doesn’t work
?Do you work?
Does she work?
I usually take the subway.
She works from home.

Present Continuous

прямо сейчас, сегодня, на этой неделе
nowright nowtodaythis weekLook!Listen!
+I am working
he / she / it is working
you / we / they are working
−I’m not working
she isn’t working
?Are you working?
Is she working?
But today I’m taking a cab.
Look! The sun is shining.
Не теряй am / is / are! She waiting → She is waiting · The food very expensive → The food is very expensive
Verbs that don’t like -ing 🚫

Эти глаголы — не действие, а состояние. Поэтому они остаются в Present Simple, даже если «прямо сейчас»: I want a coffee now, а не I’m wanting.
